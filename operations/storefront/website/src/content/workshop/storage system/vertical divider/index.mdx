## Design

A flat divider that drops into the slots on the horizontal inlay. Place them at any slot position to create compartments of any width. Different divider placements on upper and lower inlays allow tapered tool storage.

### Key features

- **Friction fit** — slides into inlay slots and stays put, but easy to reposition
- **Variable spacing** — place dividers at any slot to match your tool widths
- **Works in pairs** — upper and lower dividers can be at different positions to accommodate tapered shapes
